## Video direction

Graphite canvas, white headline, restrained blue emphasis, red up/buy and green down/sell.
Six 120-BPM-aligned beats; hard cuts at section boundaries, smooth focus moves inside each shot.
Actual captured screens are the base; only the animated historical chart is rebuilt from frozen evidence.
Reveal each line, node and proof on its visual/subtitle cue, then allow a short held read. No motionless feature slide.
Maximum idle budget 1.6s before the final held close. Captions occupy the bottom 14%; content stays above them.
No fictitious profits, live-order claims, neon holograms, tiny scrolling code, or unrelated generated footage.
No narration. Chinese captions are timed to the visual cues. The original synthesized score is the only audio; music:none prevents catalog replacement.
